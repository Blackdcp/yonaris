# Marketing privacy-request email SOP

This procedure applies to website emails whose subject starts with `Yonaris privacy request` or `Yonaris 隐私请求` and whose body contains the manual privacy-action marker.

1. Treat the request as a manual privacy or deletion review. The website form does not delete records automatically and this procedure does not promise a fixed completion time.
2. Use the submitted contact and company details to identify the earlier website request. If the match or requested scope is unclear, contact the requester through the channel they submitted before changing records.
3. Check the marketing recipient mailbox and related mailbox folders for matching website submissions. Remove or restrict matching records as appropriate for the request and applicable record-keeping duties.
4. Check Resend message records or logs when operational access is available. Delete or restrict matching provider records using available controls; if an action is unavailable, record that limitation and escalate it to the responsible operator for provider follow-up.
5. Reply through the submitted contact channel with the action taken, any record that could not be changed, and the reason. Do not expose internal recipients, credentials, or unrelated personal data.
6. Keep only a minimal completion record: completion date, request type, systems checked, outcome, any unresolved limitation, and the responsible operational role. Do not duplicate the requester’s submitted details into the completion record.

Privacy-request messages must not be routed as ordinary sales follow-up. Access is limited to operators who need it to identify and act on the request.
