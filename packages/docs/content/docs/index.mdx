## What is Yonaris?

Yonaris is a self-hosted **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** platform. It monitors how AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, and Google AI surfaces mention your brand in their responses.

With Yonaris, you own your deployment data, control your infrastructure, and can inspect how each metric is produced.

## Key Features

- **AI Visibility Tracking** — Monitor your brand's presence across ChatGPT, Claude, and Google AI Overviews
- **Citation Analysis** — See which sources AI models cite when discussing your brand
- **Competitor Intelligence** — Track how competitors appear in AI responses
- **Prompt Management** — Configure and track prompts relevant to your brand with an AI-powered wizard
- **Automated Reports** — Generate comprehensive AI visibility reports
- **White Label** — Deploy under your own brand with full multi-org support

## How It Works

Yonaris runs as a Docker Compose stack on your infrastructure. Once running, it periodically queries AI models with your configured prompts and tracks how they mention your brand, competitors, and cited sources over time.
